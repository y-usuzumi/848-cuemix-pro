- [x] Meter channel encoding: each big-endian u16 meter word is two independent u8 attenuation magnitudes, not one Q8.8 value. The high byte is the first/left channel, the low byte is the second/right channel, values `0x00` through `0xfe` use 0.5 dB attenuation steps, and `0xff` is silence. Repeated values such as `0xb4b4` initially hid the channel structure; live unequal values such as `0x6a6f`, exact per-channel path selectors, and the line-output path pairs make it explicit. Comparing a microphone tap against CueMix Pro establishes the scale: a raw value around 48 aligns with −24 dBFS, not −48 dBFS.
